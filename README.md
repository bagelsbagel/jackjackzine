## iterations

A few ideas were discarded, transformed, or revived through multiple iterations. The webzine is intended to be a non-linear narrative in the form of a spiral, but number markers were added to nudge the reader to engage with jack-jack's stories in a recommended order. Some stories were also pared down or edited out for clarity. Styling updates and new interactions were introduced later on, like the spiral animation and image effects on hover.

## reflection

I joined Zine-Making for the Web at a time when I wanted to abandon my computer completely and go live in the woods. I was in a world where technology was only corporate, or dystopian, but my teachers and peers revived my hope for technology as a conduit for novel ideas and stories. This was one of the most challenging projects I've worked on, as a non-technical artist with a preference for the analog and hand-crafted. jack-jack's story is also one that I've been wanting to tell for a while, but never found the right medium for. I'm happily surprised by how this zine turned out, and hope that readers can resonate with jack-jack's story and legacy.
