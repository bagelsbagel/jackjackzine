jack-jack짹짹

짹짹 (jack-jack) is the sound a bird makes in Korean. A bird says 짹짹 when it sings, when it calls its friends, or when it sounds an alarm.

***

짹짹 is a crow who came into our lives on a random day in May. We spent 2 whole months with him - every day a new surprise at how much love, hope, and dreaming we had for one baby crow.
